Talk to Us

Frenchie Hub is a small content site and we like hearing from readers directly. If something we wrote is wrong, outdated, or missing an important nuance — drop us a note. We check all messages and act on corrections quickly.

Got a Frenchie question we haven't covered? That's useful to hear too. The Frenchie Hub Content Team uses reader input to decide where to focus next, and questions that show up repeatedly tend to become articles.

Send Us a Message

Reach the Frenchie Hub Content Team at: hello@french-bulldog-hub.github.io

When writing about a specific guide, include the article URL so we can get it to the right person quickly. For general French Bulldog questions — especially anything about breathing, heat, or surgery options — write as much detail as you have. Context helps.

We do not respond to sponsored content or guest post pitches.
