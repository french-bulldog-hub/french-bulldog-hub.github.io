Who We Are

Frenchie Hub is a French Bulldog content site that tries to cut through the Instagram aesthetics and give owners actual, useful information. The breed has exploded in popularity — and so has the misinformation. We are here to provide a clearer picture.

Real Talk About Frenchies

French Bulldogs are genuinely wonderful companions — compact, adaptable, and full of personality. They are also one of the more expensive breeds to own when you account for potential health costs, and they have specific care requirements that not every source bothers to explain honestly. Frenchie Hub was built to be that honest source.

Our Frenchie Hub Content Team covers the questions that real Frenchie owners ask: about colors and genetics, breathing and heat risk, costs and insurance, stairs and exercise, city living logistics. We do not shy away from the harder conversations — like what BOAS surgery involves, or what "rare" colors actually mean genetically.

How Our Content Gets Made

The Frenchie Hub Content Team handles research, writing, and editorial review. We work as a team rather than individual contributors, which means every article gets a second look before it is published. Nobody rushes content out the door without it being checked first.

We aim to keep guides current — French Bulldog health research moves, breed standards shift, and product recommendations go stale. When a guide needs updating, we update it. Leaving outdated content live because it ranks well is not something we do.

What We Focus On

  • Brachycephalic airway concerns — recognizing problems, understanding surgical options
  • Heat risk management in urban and apartment environments
  • French Bulldog color genetics and what "exotic" colors actually mean for health
  • Honest cost breakdowns for prospective owners
  • Stair safety, exercise limits, and daily movement for this breed
  • Feeding, weight management, and the specific digestive issues Frenchies can develop

Our Approach to Trust

Frenchie Hub does not invent credentials or create fictional expert personas. The content team is the content team — no fabricated vets, no invented certifications. Our authority comes from research quality and factual accuracy, not from biographical embellishment.

We use affiliate links for relevant products and that is disclosed on our Advertising Notice page. For our content standards, see How We Create Content. If you want to reach us, the Contact page is always open.
