How We Create Content

French Bulldog ownership comes with a distinctive set of questions, and the Frenchie Hub Content Team has designed its content process around answering those questions accurately. This page walks through how we get from a topic idea to a published guide.

Topic Selection

We choose topics based on what French Bulldog owners are actually asking — not what generates traffic or fills a publishing calendar. That means covering genuinely useful subjects: breathing assessments at home, what BOAS staging means in practice, how to evaluate a Frenchie's heat risk on a specific day. When readers contact us with questions we haven't covered, that goes into the topic pipeline.

Research Process

The Frenchie Hub Content Team researches topics by consulting published veterinary sources, French Bulldog breed health surveys, and guidance from organisations that track brachycephalic breed health outcomes. AI tools are sometimes used in the research and drafting stage to accelerate the structuring of information — but everything produced by AI tools is reviewed, fact-checked, and edited before it becomes a published guide. We do not publish AI output unchecked.

Internal Review

Every guide is reviewed by a second member of the content team before going live. The review checks for factual accuracy, logical consistency, and tone. For health-related guides — particularly anything about breathing, exercise limits, or temperature — the reviewer specifically looks for language that could be misread as more permissive than intended. Frenchies have real health risks and we don't want our tone to soften the significance of that.

Keeping Guides Current

Guides are reviewed and updated periodically. Frenchie Hub prioritises updating guides where medical guidance, breed health data, or product availability has changed since the article was written. Updates are noted on the article page. If you find something outdated, the Contact page is the fastest way to flag it.

Revenue and Independence

Frenchie Hub uses affiliate links and that is disclosed separately on our Advertising Notice page. The content team makes editorial decisions without regard to which topics, products, or positions would generate the most affiliate revenue. If a product is not worth recommending for a French Bulldog, it will not be recommended regardless of whether a commission is available.
