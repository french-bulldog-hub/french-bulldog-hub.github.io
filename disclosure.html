Advertising Notice

Frenchie Hub earns revenue through affiliate links on Amazon. Some product links in our guides will direct you to Amazon, and if you purchase through those links, we may receive a commission. Here is everything you need to know about that arrangement.

How Affiliate Links Work

An affiliate link is a trackable URL that identifies Frenchie Hub as the source of a referral to Amazon. When you click one and make a qualifying purchase, Amazon pays us a commission — typically a small percentage of the sale. Your price as a buyer does not change. You pay exactly what Amazon charges regardless of whether the traffic came from an affiliate link or direct search.

What This Means for Our Recommendations

Frenchie Hub does not adjust its recommendations based on commission rates or affiliate availability. Products are included in guides when they are relevant and useful for French Bulldog owners. If the best harness for a Frenchie doesn't have an affiliate link, we will link to it anyway or describe it without a link. Commission potential is not a factor in editorial choices.

No brand pays to appear in our content. No manufacturer has a commercial relationship with the Frenchie Hub Content Team that would influence what we write about their products.

Legal Compliance

This disclosure satisfies the requirements of the Federal Trade Commission (16 CFR Part 255). Frenchie Hub participates in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

No Sponsored Posts

Frenchie Hub does not publish content that has been paid for by an advertiser. All guides are written and published under the sole editorial authority of the Frenchie Hub Content Team. If that ever changes, it will be labelled clearly as sponsored content.
