Size, weight and growth

How Big Do French Bulldogs Really Get? Real Owner Weights vs the AKC 28 lb Limit

The show-ring number, the vet-clinic numbers and what owners actually put on the scale, side by side.

Updated October 18, 2026 · Frenchie Hub Content Team

Ask how big French Bulldogs get and most answers stop at "under 28 pounds", the AKC show limit. Real adult Frenchies often weigh more, and many of them still have a healthy frame and visible waist. The owners who get this right are the ones who can read two things together: the number on the scale and the shape of the dog in front of them. This guide gives you both, using standards, a 2,228-dog veterinary dataset and 116 weights shared by owners.

Quick answer: Most adult French Bulldogs weigh roughly 20 to 35 pounds and stand roughly 9.5 to 14 inches at the shoulder under the FCI height range. In a large UK veterinary study, adult males averaged 13.7 kg (about 30 lb) and females 11.5 kg (about 25 lb). The AKC's 28 lb figure is a disqualification line for the show ring, not a health limit. Many pet males go over it, so judge your own dog by body condition and growth stage as well as by pounds.

What does the AKC 28 lb limit actually mean?

It is a show-ring rule. The AKC breed standard says weight is "not to exceed 28 pounds; over 28 pounds is a disqualification", which means a heavier dog cannot be judged in conformation. It says nothing about what is healthy or normal for a pet.

The same standard asks for a dog that is "muscular, heavy bone" and that looks "compact, well balanced and in good proportion." That pairing matters. A Frenchie is meant to be dense, so a dog can weigh more than its size suggests and still be in good shape. One owner in the thread we analyzed put it simply: "Frenchies are so dense."

The AKC sets a maximum, not a target

The American standard gives no ideal weight and no minimum. It gives only an upper limit. So "the AKC says 28 pounds" gets repeated as if it were an average. It is really a ceiling for one purpose. A 24 lb female and a 27 lb male can both be correct for the standard, and a 31 lb pet male can be a well-built family dog that would simply never enter a show.

How big do French Bulldogs get under other breed standards?

Other kennel clubs describe the breed in different ways. The British club gives ideal weights by sex. The international FCI standard gives a weight range and a height range, and treats size outside those limits as a disqualifying fault.

The Royal Kennel Club standard lists an ideal of 12.5 kg (28 lb) for dogs and 11 kg (24 lb) for bitches. It adds that "soundness not to be sacrificed to smallness", a direct warning against breeding ever-tinier dogs. The FCI standard No. 101 allows 9 to 14 kg for males and 8 to 13 kg for females, plus 500 g when the dog is typical of the breed. It also gives heights at the withers of 27 to 35 cm for males and 24 to 32 cm for females, with 1 cm of tolerance either way.

How much do real French Bulldogs weigh?

Real adult Frenchies are, on average, right at or above the AKC limit. The best large dataset comes from UK veterinary records. In it, adult males averaged about 30 lb and females about 25 lb, and more than half of the adult males weighed 13 kg (28.7 lb) or more.

The veterinary record numbers

The VetCompass study "Demography and disorders of the French Bulldog population under primary veterinary care in the UK in 2013" covered 2,228 French Bulldogs from 304 clinics. Its authors defined adult bodyweight as the mean of all weights recorded after 18 months of age. The mean adult bodyweight was 12.7 kg overall (about 28.0 lb), 13.7 kg for males and 11.5 kg for females. Here is how the adults with a recorded weight were spread across the study's bands:

What owners report

For a US-flavored check, we went through a June 2026 r/Frenchbulldogs thread asking "How big are your frenchies?" and logged every adult weight posted in a top-level reply. We left out puppies, English Bulldogs and four obvious jokes or mixes (62, 87 and 152 lb, and one "31 kg"). That left 116 weights. Their median was exactly 28 lb, the mean was 30 lb and the range ran from 17 to 54 lb. In all, 53 dogs (46%) were over the AKC limit. The split looked like this: 5% under 20 lb, 27% from 20 to 24.9 lb, 22% from 25 to 28 lb, 18% just over 28 up to 34.9 lb, 19% from 35 to 44.9 lb and 9% at 45 lb or more.

Treat this as a snapshot, not a survey. The thread was started by the owner of a 50 lb dog, which probably drew in owners of bigger dogs. Several posters also said their vet had asked them to slim their dog down. Even so, the middle of the sample matches the veterinary data closely: the typical adult sits around 25 to 30 lb.

When do French Bulldogs stop growing?

Most of the growth happens in the first year, but weight keeps creeping up after that. The VetCompass growth curves show French Bulldog puppies "grow rapidly during their first year but continue to gain further weight up to three years." They were built from 3,413 weight records for 684 females and 5,276 records for 783 males.

So a 12-month weight is not the final word. A dog that reaches 24 lb at its first birthday can fill out into a broader adult. Ask whether the extra pounds are chest and muscle filling out or fat collecting over the ribs and at the base of the tail. Only your hands can tell you that, and the next section shows how.

What changes between one and three years

  • Expect smaller gains. The study found rapid growth in year one and continued gains after it, so some weight change in years two and three is expected.
  • Pair every weigh-in with a body check. That is the only way to tell whether new weight is muscle filling out or fat collecting.
  • Review portions at the switch to adult food. Ask your vet to set a portion and a target weight for your dog rather than carrying puppy amounts forward.

Is my Frenchie overweight or just big-boned?

You find out with your hands, not the scale. Feel the ribs, look for a waist from above and check for a tuck behind the rib cage. A dog that passes all three checks is in good condition at 22 lb or 32 lb. A dog that fails them is carrying extra fat at any weight.

Guide Dogs for the Blind's body condition guide explains the 9-point scale that vets use, where 4 to 5 counts as ideal. At an ideal score the ribs are easy to feel with light pressure under a thin fat layer, "similar to how the back of your hand feels." The waist is visible from above, and the abdomen tucks up behind the rib cage. By a score of 6 the ribs need firmer pressure and the waist is barely visible.

Why the Frenchie shape can fool you

A short, wide body and a big head can make a lean Frenchie look stocky. A thick coat isn't the issue with this breed. The issue is that the outline looks "chunky" by design. That is why a touch test beats a glance at photos online. If your hands find a thin fat layer over the ribs and a clear waist, the dog is built big, not fat. If they don't, the scale number matters less than the trend. Your vet can give you a target weight for your own dog.

How to measure and track your Frenchie's size: a step-by-step routine

Owners who track size well do the same few things every month. It takes about ten minutes, and after half a year you will know your dog's normal range better than any chart can.

  1. Weigh the same way each time. Step on a bathroom scale alone, then holding your dog, and subtract. Use the same scale, at the same time of day, before a meal.
  2. Write down the number in both units. Writing "12.4 kg / 27.3 lb" makes it easy to compare with UK or FCI numbers later.
  3. Do the hands-on check. Ribs, waist from above, tuck from the side. Note it as "thin", "ideal" or "heavy" next to the weight.
  4. Measure height once in a while. Stand your dog square on a hard floor, lay a flat object across the top of the shoulder blades and measure down to the floor. The FCI ranges are 27 to 35 cm (about 10.5 to 14 in) for males and 24 to 32 cm (about 9.5 to 12.5 in) for females.
  5. Compare month to month, not dog to dog. A steady weight with an ideal body check is the goal. A slow climb with a fading waist is the early warning.
  6. Bring the log to checkups. A dated list of weights and body checks gives your vet far more to work with than a single weigh-in.
  7. Re-check gear when the number changes. Harnesses, crates and travel gear have their own limits. If your adult dog has filled out, re-measure before trusting the old fit, as we explain in our French Bulldog car seat sizing guide.

Common mistakes owners make about French Bulldog size

  • Treating 28 lb as a health line. It is a show disqualification. A lean 30 lb male is not "too big", and a soft 26 lb female is not automatically fine.
  • Judging adult size at one year. The veterinary curves show weight gain continuing up to three years, so expect some filling out.
  • Comparing your dog with strangers online. Owner-shared weights run from 17 to 54 lb. Your dog's own trend tells you more.
  • Buying size in a sales pitch. Labels like "micro", "mini" or "XL" are marketing terms. None of the three standards above recognizes them.
  • Weighing on different scales. Two different scales can give two different readings. Pick one for your log and stick with it.
  • Ignoring the body check because the number looks fine. Pounds alone cannot tell muscle from fat in a breed built to be dense.

Do male and female French Bulldogs differ in size?

Yes. On average, males run noticeably heavier. In the UK veterinary data adult males averaged 13.7 kg and females 11.5 kg, a gap of about 5 lb. Both the British and FCI standards also set separate figures for each sex.

The ranges overlap a lot, though. More than a third of UK females (37.7%) fell in the 11 to 13 kg band, which was also the largest male band (30.7%). So a big female can easily outweigh a small male. Frequently asked questions

How big do French Bulldogs get when fully grown?

Most adults weigh about 20 to 35 pounds and stand roughly 9.5 to 14 inches at the shoulder, the FCI height range for both sexes. In UK veterinary records, adult males averaged 13.7 kg (about 30 lb) and females 11.5 kg (about 25 lb). Individual dogs fall well outside those averages in both directions.

Is a 30 lb French Bulldog too big?

Not necessarily. Thirty pounds is over the AKC show limit of 28 lb but inside the FCI range for males, which runs to 14 kg (about 31 lb). Whether it suits your dog depends on body condition: ribs easy to feel under a thin layer, a visible waist and an abdominal tuck.

What happens if my Frenchie is over 28 pounds?

In AKC conformation shows, a dog over 28 pounds is disqualified. For a pet, nothing follows automatically from the number. Use a hands-on body condition check and ask your vet for a target weight for your own dog.

At what age is a French Bulldog full size?

Most growth happens in the first year, but weight keeps rising after that: the VetCompass growth curves show French Bulldogs continue to gain weight up to about three years of age.

Are male French Bulldogs bigger than females?

On average, yes. UK data put adult males about 2.2 kg (roughly 5 lb) heavier than females. The ranges overlap a lot, so the parents' size is a better guide to a puppy's adult size than its sex.

Are "mini" or "XL" French Bulldogs a real size category?

No. The AKC, Royal Kennel Club and FCI standards describe one breed with one size range. "Mini", "micro" and "XL" are seller labels, not recognized varieties, so ask for the parents' adult weights instead.

Know the number, read the dog

So how big do French Bulldogs get? A typical adult is about 25 to 30 lb, males run heavier than females, and plenty of healthy pets sit above the AKC's 28 lb show line. The skill that sets a sharp owner apart is pairing a steady weight log with a monthly hands-on check. Do that, and you will know whether your Frenchie is big, fit or quietly drifting, long before a chart could tell you.
